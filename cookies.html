Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains the small files and browser storage dbeat uses. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings in the footer of the home page.

Essential (always on)

These make dbeat work. They are not used for tracking, and we do not need consent for them.

NameWhat it doesLasts
Supabase sign-in token (browser storage)Keeps you signed inUntil you sign out, or the session expires
dbeat.langRemembers English or HindiUntil you clear it
dbeat.themeRemembers light or dark modeUntil you clear it
dbeat.consentRemembers your cookie choiceUntil you clear it
dbeat.companyHolds your company name for a moment while you confirm your emailRemoved after your account is set up

Analytics (only if you choose "Allow analytics")

If you allow it, we load Microsoft Clarity. It records clicks, scrolling and screen recordings so we can find confusing screens. We set Clarity to its strictest masking ("Strict"), which hides on-screen text and typed values in the recordings. Clarity sets cookies such as _clck, _clsk, CLID, ANONCHK, MR, SM and MUID. Microsoft's own terms and privacy statement also apply to that data. If you choose "No thanks", Clarity is never loaded and none of those cookies are set.

Advertising and marketing

We do not use advertising or marketing cookies.

Fonts

We load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to download them. Your IP address is visible to Google in that request.

Changing your mind

  • Use Cookie settings on the home page to allow or refuse analytics again. Choosing "No thanks" after allowing removes the analytics cookies.
  • You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser settings.
