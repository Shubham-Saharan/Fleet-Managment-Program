Copyright and Takedown

Last updated: 24 November 2026

We respect other people's intellectual property and ask everyone who uses dbeat to do the same.

What users can add to dbeat

dbeat does not host public content. People enter text such as vehicle names, trip notes and driver details, and that content is visible only to members of their own company. It is not published to other customers or to the public. Because of this, copyright problems are unlikely, but we have a process anyway.

Our own intellectual property

The dbeat software, design, text, logos and name belong to dbeat or its licensors and are protected by copyright and other laws. You may not copy or reuse them without written permission. See the Terms of Service.

Reporting an infringement

If you believe material in dbeat infringes your copyright, email ylc27shubham.saharan@mastersunion.org with:

  1. Your name, address, email and phone, and your signature (typing your full name is enough).
  2. A description of the work you say is infringed, and where you published or registered it.
  3. Where the material is in dbeat, with enough detail for us to find it (for example, company or page and the text concerned). Because content is private to each company, tell us how you know it is there.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by you, your agent or the law.
  5. A statement that the information is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.

We will review the notice promptly, and if it is valid we will remove or disable the material and tell the account holder. We may ask for more information.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed and you believe that was a mistake, email us with your name and contact details, a description of the material and where it was, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake or misidentification, and your signature. We will pass it to the person who complained. If they do not tell us they have started legal action within 10 business days, we may restore the material.

Repeat infringers

We will suspend or close accounts of people who repeatedly infringe copyright.

Which law and which agent

dbeat is operated from India and serves customers in India. We handle notices under India's Copyright Act, 1957 (including section 52(1)(c)) and the Information Technology Act, 2000. We follow the same practical steps used under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and will respond to DMCA-style notices sent to the address above. We have not registered a designated agent with the US Copyright Office, because we do not operate from or target the United States. If that changes, we will register one and list it here.

Making a false claim can have legal consequences, including liability for damages.
