Data Processing Agreement

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This agreement forms part of the Terms of Service between dbeat ("Processor", "we") and the company that uses dbeat ("Customer"). It applies automatically whenever we process personal data on the Customer's behalf. It follows GDPR Art. 28 and the equivalent duties of a data processor under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Roles

The Customer is the controller (data fiduciary) of the personal data it enters into dbeat, for example drivers' names, phone numbers, addresses, licence and ID details. dbeat is the processor. For account and billing data about the Customer's own staff, dbeat is a controller; see the Privacy Policy.

2. What we process, and why

  • Subject: providing the dbeat Service to the Customer.
  • Duration: while the Customer has an account, plus the deletion period in section 9.
  • People concerned: the Customer's drivers and staff, and any other people the Customer enters.
  • Types of data: name, phone, home address, driving licence number, class and expiry, ID type and number, ID-check status, and trip and vehicle records linked to them.
  • Purpose: storing, displaying and organising the data so the Customer can schedule trips and keep records.

3. Our commitments

  • We process the data only on the Customer's documented instructions (these terms and use of the Service), unless the law requires otherwise, in which case we will tell the Customer first where allowed.
  • People who can access the data are bound by confidentiality, and access is limited to what is needed to run and secure the Service.
  • We apply the security measures described on our Security page.
  • We tell the Customer without undue delay (we aim for within 72 hours) after confirming a breach affecting the Customer's personal data, and give the facts it needs to meet its own duties.
  • We help the Customer answer requests from the people concerned (access, correction, deletion and so on), and help with impact assessments and consultations where reasonably needed. Reasonable costs for unusually large requests may apply.
  • On request we give the Customer the information needed to show we follow this agreement, and allow reasonable audits where the information given is not enough, on reasonable notice, during business hours, at the Customer's cost, and subject to confidentiality.

4. The Customer's commitments

The Customer has a lawful basis and any required consent or notice for the data it enters, keeps the data accurate and limited to what it needs, and gives only lawful instructions.

6. Transfers

Data is stored in Ireland and may be processed in other countries by sub-processors. We rely on their published data-protection terms and on appropriate transfer safeguards where the law requires them.

7. Personal data breaches

See section 3. We will describe, as far as we know, what happened, what data and people are affected, likely consequences, and what we are doing about it.

8. Assistance and records

We keep a record of our processing activities for the Customer's data and make the relevant parts available on request.

9. Deletion and return

When the Customer's account ends, or on request, we return the data in a common format on request and then delete it from live systems within 30 days. Backups, where they exist, expire within 30 days after that. We may keep what the law requires us to keep, and will keep it protected.

10. Liability and law

Liability under this agreement is subject to the limits in the Terms of Service. Indian law governs it. If the Customer is subject to the GDPR or UK GDPR and needs additional contractual clauses for a transfer, contact us and we will sign them.
