Security

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page describes the protections we use today. We do not hold a security certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2, and we do not claim one.

How we protect your data

  • Encrypted connections. The site and all traffic to our database use HTTPS (TLS). Browsers are told to use HTTPS only.
  • Company separation. Each company can see only its own data. This is enforced inside the database (row-level security), not just in the app, and only owners and dispatchers can change fleet data.
  • Sign-in. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes by our authentication provider. Email addresses are verified. You can also sign in with Google, so we never handle a Google password. Signing up is rate limited.
  • Payments. Card details go straight to our payment provider and never reach us. Payment notifications to our server are checked with a cryptographic signature and rejected if they do not match.
  • Secrets stay on the server. Keys that can read or change data are held in the hosting provider's protected settings, never in the website code or the browser.
  • Browser protections. We send security headers (a content security policy, clickjacking protection, no content sniffing, and others) and check the integrity of the outside library we load.
  • Input handling. Text typed into the app is escaped before it is shown, and saved data is checked before it is displayed.
  • Encrypted storage. Our database provider encrypts stored data at rest.
  • Access. Access to production systems is limited to the people who need it, and our providers keep access logs.
  • Sensitive data. Driver ID numbers are partly hidden on screen.
  • Logs. Server logs are short-lived and we avoid putting personal data in them.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a security problem, please email ylc27shubham.saharan@mastersunion.org with the details and steps to reproduce it. Please give us a reasonable time to fix it before telling anyone else.

  • We will acknowledge your report within 5 business days and keep you updated.
  • Please do not access other people's data, disrupt the service, or run automated attacks. Test only with your own account.
  • If you follow these rules, we will not take legal action against you for good-faith research.
  • We do not currently run a paid bug bounty, but we are happy to thank you publicly if you wish.

If something goes wrong

We follow an incident procedure: contain the problem, find what was affected, fix it, tell affected customers without undue delay, tell authorities where the law requires, and write down what we learned. See the Privacy Policy and Data Processing Agreement.

What you can do

  • Use a long, unique password, or sign in with Google.
  • Give access only to people who need it, and remove people who leave.
  • Enter only the driver details you need.
