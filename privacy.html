Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

dbeat ("we", "us") is a fleet and dispatch scheduling web app for transport companies, at dbeat.co.in. dbeat is operated from India. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, who else handles it, and your rights. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, and also follows the principles of the EU and UK GDPR for anyone those laws protect.

1. Two roles: our customers and us

  • Account data (the sign-in details and company details of the people who use dbeat): we decide why and how it is used. We are the controller (data fiduciary).
  • Fleet data (vehicles, trips, and details of a customer's drivers): the customer company decides what to enter and why. The customer is the controller, and we process it on the customer's behalf as a processor. See the Data Processing Agreement.

5. Transfers outside your country

Our providers run systems in several countries, so data can be processed outside India or your home country. We choose providers that publish data-protection terms and security practices, and we send only what each one needs. Customers in India should know that account and fleet data is stored in Ireland.

7. Your rights and how to use them (data deletion included)

You can ask us to: tell you what we hold; correct it; delete it; give you a copy in a common format; limit or stop certain uses; withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect earlier use); and nominate someone to exercise your rights if you cannot. Email ylc27shubham.saharan@mastersunion.org and we will answer within 30 days. We may need to confirm who you are first. For driver data held by a customer company, we will pass your request to that company, as it is the controller.

Inside the app, a company owner can edit or delete vehicles, drivers and trips at any time. To delete a whole account, email us.

Complaints. Please write to ylc27shubham.saharan@mastersunion.org first (we act as the grievance contact) and give us a chance to fix it. You may also complain to your data protection authority, such as the Data Protection Board of India, or your local supervisory authority if you are in the EU or UK.

8. Security

We protect data with encrypted connections, separation between companies enforced in the database, hashed passwords, restricted staff access, signed payment notifications and security headers. See Security for detail. No system is perfectly secure.

9. Data breaches

If we learn of a breach that puts personal data at risk, we will act at once to contain it, tell affected customers without undue delay (we aim for within 72 hours of confirming it), and notify authorities and individuals where the law requires. Customers who are controllers of driver data are responsible for informing the drivers affected, and we will give them the facts they need to do so.

10. Cookies and similar storage

We use a few essential items to keep you signed in and remember your language, light or dark mode, and cookie choice. We use analytics cookies only if you accept them. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

11. Children

dbeat is for businesses. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes

We will update this policy as dbeat changes. We will change the date above, and tell customers by email or in the app if a change is important.
