Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

dbeat ("we") is a fleet and dispatch scheduling web app for transport companies, at dbeat.co.in. This page explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (we never see your password in plain text), or your name and email if you sign in with Google.
  • Company details: your company name.
  • Fleet data you enter: vehicles (names, plate numbers, fuel, document dates), trips and schedules, and drivers (name, phone, home address, licence number and expiry, ID type and number, and whether the ID was checked).
  • Usage information: how the app is used (pages opened, clicks, screen recordings of sessions) through Microsoft Clarity, plus basic device and browser details.
  • Choices stored on your device: language, light or dark mode.

Why we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your fleet and save your changes.
  • To improve dbeat: find confusing screens and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account or the service.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Driver information

If you enter details about your drivers, you decide what to enter and you are responsible for having their permission to do so. Only people in your company's account can see that information. Please enter only what you need.

Who else handles your data

  • Supabase stores accounts and fleet data (servers in Ireland).
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google provides sign-in if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Microsoft Clarity provides usage analytics and session recordings.

These providers process data for us under their own terms and privacy policies.

Keeping it safe

Every company's data is kept separate, connections are encrypted, and access is limited to signed-in members of that company. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your company's data, except where we must keep something by law.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to withdraw your consent, by contacting us below. You can also edit or delete your fleet data inside the app at any time.

Children

dbeat is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
